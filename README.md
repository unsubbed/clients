# Unsubbed Clients

**A free alternative to HoneyBook and Dubsado.** Proposals, contracts and invoices you don't pay a monthly fee for. Runs entirely in your browser. No account, no subscription, no tracking. Your client details never leave your device.

**Use it now:** https://unsubbed.github.io/clients

## What it does
- Write proposals, contracts and invoices beside a live sheet of paper that shows exactly what will print
- Turn a proposal into a contract and a contract into an invoice, with the line items carried across
- Discounts, tax, payment plans with deposits, and tick-when-paid tracking
- See what's waiting to be paid and what's overdue at a glance, per currency
- Sign by drawing, by name and date, or on the printed page
- Save any document as a PDF, in any language or alphabet your browser can show
- Keep a simple address book of clients
- Export your invoices as CSV for your bookkeeping
- Open and save your data as an ordinary file on your own device

## Not legal advice
The contract text is a plain starting point. Have a lawyer check it for your work and where you live. Signatures drawn here have no audit trail, so use a dedicated signing service for high-stakes deals.

## Privacy
Everything happens on your device. The app makes no outside network requests: no analytics, no ads, no server. Your work is stored in this browser until you clear it, and you can save a copy at any time. Data isn't encrypted in the browser, so on a shared computer use your own browser profile.

## Known limits
No online client signing, client portal, card payments, automatic reminders, recurring invoices or sync between devices: those need a server. You send the PDF yourself, and the invoice shows your own payment instructions. Editing a client or your business details changes how older documents print, so keep the PDFs you send.

## Run it yourself
It's a plain web page. Download this repository and open `index.html`, or serve the folder with any static web server.
